THE STORY

WELCOME TO TOKYO

A NORMAL LIFE ISN'T AN OPTION.

After being falsely accused of a crime, a troubled teenager is forced to transfer to Shujin Academy in Tokyo.

What begins as an ordinary school year soon turns into something far more mysterious. Alongside a group of fellow students, he discovers the Metaverse — a distorted world born from the desires of humanity.

Taking the identities of Phantom Thieves, they infiltrate Palaces, steal corrupted desires and attempt to change the hearts of those who abuse their power.
